# Planned Leave Policy (fictional sample)
Owner: People Operations | Effective: 2026-01-01 | Review: annually

## Eligibility and balance
Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of paid leave at the end of each completed month. The employee portal displays the current balance; it is the source of truth for requests. Unused days carry over up to a maximum of five days at year end. This example does not describe a real employer's benefits.

## Request and approval
Submit planned leave in the portal at least five working days before the first day away. Include the dates, the name of a colleague who can cover urgent work, and any handoff notes. The manager approves or returns the request within two working days; a submitted request is not yet approved. For unexpected absence, notify the manager as soon as practical and record the dates afterward.

## Coverage and questions
Before an approved absence, hand off open customer issues and deadlines to the named colleague. For a balance discrepancy, contact People Operations with the request ID rather than editing the balance locally.
