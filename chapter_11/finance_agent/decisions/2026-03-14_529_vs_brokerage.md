# 529 vs Taxable Brokerage - 2026-03-14

**Decision:** fund education savings through the 529.

**Why:** state tax deduction is worth more than the flexibility premium at this contribution level, and
the education use is near-certain. Withdrawal penalty risk judged low.

**Rejected:** taxable brokerage for flexibility. Rejected because the deduction compounds and the
flexibility was not actually needed for a known expense.
