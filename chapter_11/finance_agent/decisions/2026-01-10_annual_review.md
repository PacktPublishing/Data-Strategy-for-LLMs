# Annual Review - 2026-01-10

**Decision:** move target allocation 80/20 to 60/40.

**Why:** retirement horizon shortened by five years after a plan change. Sequence-of-returns risk now
outweighs the expected-return give-up. M. Okonkwo modelled both; the 60/40 path failed in fewer scenarios.

**Rejected:** a glide path stepping down 5 points per year. Rejected because it leaves the portfolio
over-weighted equity through the highest-risk window.
