# Decision Rules

- **Risk tolerance:** moderate. Target 60/40 equity/fixed income, rebalance when either side drifts >5 points.
- **Equity blackout:** no employer-equity sales between quarter-end and earnings. See `entities/employer_equity.md`.
- **Giving:** 5% of gross annually, front-loaded in Q4 for the deduction.
- **Emergency floor:** keep 6 months of expenses in cash before any new taxable investment.
